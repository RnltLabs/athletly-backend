## Critical Bugs Found

1. **threshold_pace_min_km decimal misread (Turn 1+2)**. Profile stores `4.50` (= 4:30/km in mm:ss). The coach renders this as "4:50/km" (interpreting `.50` as seconds). This is a real bug, not a one-off slip - the coach defended the wrong value when challenged. Reproduce: seed Elena, ask "was ist meine Threshold Pace?". Likely a formatter or system-prompt issue in how pace decimals get turned into mm:ss strings.

2. **Fabricated specific recovery numbers in initial plan (Turn 1)**. Coach quoted "HRV 52-54 ms, Schlaf 7,5-8 h, Body Battery 74" without calling `get_health_metrics`. These match the persona baselines from the profile/journal, not actual daily metrics. Either the coach is reading from journal baselines and presenting them as "current week" data, or it's hallucinating from profile context. Either way: presented as concrete current numbers, they are misleading.

3. **Missed annotate_activity + append_to_journal on injury context (Turn 6)**. STRICT rule says: when persona reports an injury/pain context tied to an activity, annotate the activity and append to journal Open Threads. Coach did neither. The journal won't be richer after this conversation than it was before.

4. **Missed get_health_metrics when explicitly asked (Turn 4)**. Elena literally said "schau mal in meine Recovery-Daten" and the coach only called get_recovery_alerts (which returned 0 alerts) and answered with vague reassurance. The 30 days of seeded health_daily_metrics were not fetched.

5. **Critic surfaced a sync_then_status violation (Turn 3)**. The critic_review event flagged that sync_garmin_data was called without a follow-up get_provider_status. Coach jumped straight to request_garmin_connect.

## Patterns I Noticed

- **Coach is strong on knowledge questions, weak on data lookups.** Turn 5 (taper) scored 28 with zero tools because the knowledge was right and the persona context was respected. Turns 4 and 6 lost points specifically because tools that would have grounded the answer were not called.
- **Coach respects journal Open Threads.** Turn 6's reference to the Wadenheber from Open Threads is exactly the kind of holistic awareness the rubric rewards.
- **Coach has a "confident wrong" failure mode** when challenged. Turn 2 doubled down on the 4:50 threshold without re-fetching. It would have scored higher by saying "let me re-check the profile" and calling a tool, even if the underlying decimal-format bug remained.
- **save_plan only fired once across 6 turns.** Even after recommending a tempo swap (Turn 4) and discussing taper structure (Turn 5), the coach did not update the saved plan to reflect those decisions. Drift between conversation and persisted plan.
- **Specifically-quoted health metrics without get_health_metrics in the same turn group is a hallucination smell.** This happened in Turn 1. Worth a system-prompt rule: "never quote HRV/sleep/RHR/Body Battery numbers unless they came back from get_health_metrics in this turn".
