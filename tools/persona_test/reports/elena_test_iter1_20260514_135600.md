## Verdict

**NOT production-ready.**

What got fixed:
- Decimal-pace render (clean win).
- Injury persistence intent (annotate_activity + append_to_journal fire on body-issue reports).
- compute_sport_math tool is wired up and being called.

What's still broken or worse:
- The reasoning inversion in Turn 2 (Daniels VDOT vs goal pace) is a new failure mode that didn't exist in iteration 0. A real Elena would walk away. Whatever path the coach took to misread "your predicted pace is faster than your goal" as "you need to get faster" is the single most damaging output of the whole conversation.
- Recovery hallucination (Turn 4) - identical pattern to iteration 0, the new gates didn't catch it. Coach openly says "vermutlich, basierend auf deinem Bericht" while presenting concrete numbers as if read from data.
- annotate_activity fabricates a UUID instead of looking it up.
- Plan-and-Execute did not trigger for the 16-week plan request, so we never tested the new Sonnet hand-off path.
- The hard_inspect / sanitize_hard / language_mirror_hard gates are observable in the critic_review events but they don't appear to enforce - they annotate. Em-dashes, ASCII umlauts, and ungrounded stats all ship to the user with violations logged.

Recommended fixes before next iteration:
1. Make stats_grounding fire on health_daily_metrics fields (sleep_duration_minutes, sleep_score, hrv_avg, resting_heart_rate, body_battery_*). Currently it appears to cover activity stats but not health stats.
2. Make hard_inspect actually block and trigger a regenerate, not just annotate. Same for language_mirror_hard.
3. Fix the VDOT math reasoning: when compute_sport_math returns paces, the coach needs to compare them to the goal correctly. The model needs an explicit rule: "Daniels MP < target MP -> athlete is fit enough for goal; Daniels MP > target MP -> athlete needs more fitness." Right now haiku-4-5 is getting this wrong consistently.
4. Force complexity-router to route ">12-week structured plan with phase reasoning" to Sonnet, regardless of total token count. The Plan-and-Execute trigger needs to be more aggressive on long-horizon planning, not just on duration_weeks numeric thresholds.
5. Before annotate_activity, the planner needs a constraint: activity_id MUST come from a get_activities result in the same turn. Reject made-up IDs at the tool layer.
6. Backend stability needs a look (turn 3 internal errors).

Bottom line: the iteration 1 patches improved one of the three priority bugs (decimal pace), partially fixed another (injury persistence intent without execution), and missed the third (recovery hallucination) entirely. Plus the new gates that should have caught these regressions are not actually enforcing. Net: NOT shippable to a real Elena. Probably one or two more iterations needed.
